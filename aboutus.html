ABOUT  US

Happy to see you
Happier to serve you


Treating you as our neighbor means treating you to the very best.
It’s why we pour our heart into everything we do.
It’s why pastries to use aren’t just a product, they’re our passion.
And why our bakers, cakers and baristas go further every day to perfect their own craft, serving up freshly-baked breads,
delicious coffee and cakes more than worthy of their pedestals.

We do it all to bring a smile to your face, because we believe the world needs more moments of joy.
And there’s no better place to find it than a place that feels just like home.
Where you can share good conversation over an even better bite, and where the community comes together, and life is richer for it.

Welcome to your neighborhood bakery café.
We’re so glad you’re here.

Our Vision

To reestablish the neighborhood bakery café as the heart of the community around the world.

Our Mission

To bring expertly crafted baked and brewed goods to our guests  through a warm and welcoming bakery café experience that delivers joy to everyone.

Our Values

Have Heart

A genuine feeling of warmth is infused in all that we do – from the atmosphere in our bakery café to our relationships with guests to the positive attitudes we bring to work everyday. It’s a heartfelt approach that radiates outward from our authentic desire to put a smile on peoples faces and make them feel not just welcome but invited.

Rise to the Occasion

We bring the best to every task, pouring over details and never doing anything halfway or in haste. We take care to always put our best foot forward and in turn, offer guests an irresistible experience steeped in passion and precision.


Nourish Community

As a neighborhood bakery café, we’re part of the communities we serve. As a good neighbor, we operate with integrity, honesty and respect. We look for ways big and small to make a positive impact in the lives of our guests – fostering connections, building community and making peoples days a little brighter, any way we can.


Spread Joy

There’s an inherent joy in the food we serve and the desserts we craft, and we share that joy with the world everyday. From decisions made behind a desk to smiles offered behind a counter, the desire to make the world a happier place drive everything we do.


About Us

Paris Baguette is a global bakery café brand founded in 1988 by Sang-kook Hur, under the parent company SPC Group in South Korea.
